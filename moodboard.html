01

What reads as "kart game"

Five signals a player checks for in the first two seconds. Every workstream below exists to put a checkmark on one of these.

1Kart silhouette — big wheels, small body, a driver
2Motion that reacts to input — roll, wheel spin, bounce
3A camera that sells speed
4Feedback effects — sparks, smoke, boost flame
5A game-style HUD, not default UI

04

Genre references

Touchstones for tone and technique only — Tilt's characters, tracks and names stay original.

Crash Team Racing Nitro-Fueled

Borrowing: chunky proportions, readable drift-spark color tiers

Team Sonic Racing

Borrowing: saturated flat lighting that still reads in split-screen

Sonic & All-Stars Racing Transformed

Borrowing: camera FOV push and speed-line vignette at top speed

Fall Guys

Borrowing: soft toon outlines on low-poly geometry, squash-and-stretch

Overcooked

Borrowing: party-game HUD energy — big numerals, no chrome

Sunset Overdrive

Borrowing: punchy per-scene color grading LUTs

"Mario Kart style" describes the genre only. Assets, characters and track names are original — genre reference here informs tone and technique, not content to copy.
